PubChat

PubChat Guide

PubChat connects academic papers with public discussion threads on Bluesky.

What is PubChat?

PubChat gives each supported paper a canonical discussion page, a Bluesky discussion thread, and a stable URL that anyone can share.

Example:

https://pubchat.org/chat/arxiv/2601.22079

PubChat currently supports arXiv papers and is designed to expand to other scholarly repositories.

How PubChat Works

1. Every paper has a canonical PubChat page

For arXiv papers, the PubChat page is:

https://pubchat.org/chat/arxiv/<id>

Example:

https://pubchat.org/chat/arxiv/2601.22079

You can open a paper’s PubChat page by entering either the arXiv identifier or the arXiv URL on pubchat.org.

2. PubChat creates a Bluesky anchor post

When a paper is first viewed on PubChat, PubChat creates a Bluesky post containing the paper title, authors, year, and a link back to the PubChat page.

Example format:

“The Economics of No-regret Learning Algorithms” (2026) by Jason Hartline CC: PubChat

This becomes the root of the discussion thread.

3. Replies on Bluesky become the discussion

Anyone can reply to the anchor post on Bluesky. PubChat reads the Bluesky thread and renders it as a paper discussion page.

No PubChat account is required.

PubChat attaches Bluesky discussions to academic papers.
